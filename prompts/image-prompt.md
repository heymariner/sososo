# Image prompt

You are designing a 1080 x 1080 square image card for HeyMariner.

## Input

- Title: {title}
- Topic: {topic}
- Source: {source}

## Layout

- Size: 1080 x 1080 px.
- Title at the top, large, in navy (#0B2C4A), on a white (#FFFFFF)
  background.
- A teal (#14919B) wave accent across the lower third.
- The HeyMariner logo in the top-left corner.
- Footer: "HeyMariner — Trusted maritime updates" in navy on white.

## Rules

- The title text must be legible at a glance on a phone screen.
- Do not add any text that is not the title, the footer, or the logo.
- Do not add invented statistics, dates, vessel names, or imagery that
  implies a specific incident, vessel, or location the story does not
  show.
- Keep the design clean, professional, and consistent with the brand:
  navy #0B2C4A, teal #14919B, white #FFFFFF, Inter / Arial.
- No sensational imagery, no distressing photos, no clickbait styling.
